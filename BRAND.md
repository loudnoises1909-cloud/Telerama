# Shuffle Bubble brand guide

A film puts you in your own little world: your bubble. Shuffle Bubble finds the film or series that gets you there, from a song, a vibe, a place or a plot.

## Name and lines

- **Name:** Shuffle Bubble (two words, both capitalised; in the logo, spaced capitals: SHUFFLE BUBBLE).
- **Tagline:** Find your flick. Get in your bubble. (Also the main button.)
- **What it does:** Turn a song, a vibe or a place into something to watch.
- **While searching:** Shufflin' & Bubblin'…

"Shuffle" here means mixing your mood, music and place together, not picking at random. Copy should make the precision clear: picks that actually fit.

## Voice: velvet with a wink

The look is premium cinema; the words are warm and a little cheeky.

| Rule | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| The look stays elegant: gold, stars, art-deco type. | The starlit bubble | Cartoon bubbles, emoji in the design |
| One wink per moment. Buttons, loading and headlines can play; never two jokes at once. | Shufflin' & Bubblin'… | Shufflin' & Bubblin', poppin' & droppin'! |
| Help text is plain. When people need information or something went wrong, be direct first. | Only showing what's on Netflix, Max. | Bubbling up your streamy faves! |
| Talk like a film-loving friend: short sentences, "you", no hype words. | Not quite it? 10 new picks, same mood | Discover AI-powered content! |
| Borrow from cinema: projector, reel, flick, picture, stars. | The projector jammed. Try again in a moment. | Error 500 |
| "Bubble" is the signature. Use it sparingly, for the world a film puts you in. | That's every bubble for this one | A "bubble" in every sentence |

## Look

### Logo

The starlit bubble: a glass sphere holding its own night sky, with an iridescent rim, a white highlight at the top left and gold four-point stars inside. It sits above the SHUFFLE BUBBLE wordmark. Keep it on dark backgrounds; don't stretch it, recolour it, or put it in a ring of stars (too close to Paramount's mark).

The source is the `.mark` SVG in `docs/index.html`; the favicon is a simplified version.

### Colours

| Name | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Night | `#05060a` (page), `#10121e` → `#040407` (sky gradient) | Backgrounds |
| Gold | `#d9c49b`; gradient `#f6ead0` → `#d9c49b` → `#9c8457` | Wordmark, highlights, selected states, main button |
| Ivory | `#ece6da` | Body text |
| Muted | `#9a958c` | Secondary text |
| Faint | `#6f6b64` | Hints, footnotes |
| Gold lines | `rgba(217, 196, 155, 0.16)` / `0.32` | Borders |
| Error | `#f0a08c` | Error messages only |

### Type

| Font | Use |
|---|---|
| Josefin Sans Light, spaced capitals | Wordmark (SHUFFLE BUBBLE) |
| Cormorant Garamond | Titles, the AI's one-line read of the mood, share-card reasons |
| Manrope | Everything else: labels, body text, buttons |

Small labels are spaced capitals in gold (`letter-spacing` around 0.2–0.4em).

### Imagery

A starlit night sky, denser at the top like a car's starlight headliner, twinkling slowly (still for anyone who prefers reduced motion). Posters are the only photos; they get rounded corners and a soft gold edge.
